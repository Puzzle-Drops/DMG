Here's a practical guide for navigating that card shop as a non-Japanese speaker!

What you're looking for

In Japan, Dark Magician Girl is called "ブラック・マジシャン・ガール" (Burakku Majishan Gāru). Show that to staff on your phone — they'll know exactly what you mean.

The "Dark Magician Girl" archetype is a sub-archetype of the broader "Dark Magician" and "Magician Girl" archetypes. She also has eight different variant card arts, making her one of the most collected single cards in the game.

Tips for navigating the shop

    Show staff your phone with this text: ブラック・マジシャン・ガール のカードをすべて探しています ("I'm looking for all Dark Magician Girl cards")
    Single card binders — most Japanese card shops organize singles in plastic-sleeve binders by set or rarity. Ask to flip through the "Dark Magician" section.
    Look for the character — her artwork is very distinctive (a blonde girl in a blue/pink outfit with a magic staff), so you can visually scan binder pages even without reading Japanese.
    Rarity variants — the same card can appear in Common, Rare, Ultra Rare, Secret Rare, and Prismatic Secret Rare versions. Prices vary hugely. Decide beforehand if you want one copy of each card or every rarity variant.
    Ask about the "OCG" packs — Japan uses the OCG (Original Card Game) format, which has exclusive prints not sold in the West. This is actually a great opportunity to find Japan-only versions.

Phrase to show staff on your phone:

    ブラック・マジシャン・ガール のカードを探しています。全種類ありますか？
    (I'm looking for Dark Magician Girl cards. Do you have all types?)

Ocg yes. How do i find ocg?

In Japan, all Yu-Gi-Oh! cards sold there are OCG — you don't need to do anything special. Every card in that shop is already OCG.

The distinction only matters if you were back home, where cards are sold in the TCG (international) format. Since you're in Hokkaido right now, everything on the shelves is the real deal.

A couple of OCG-specific things to watch for:

    Japanese text only — OCG cards are in Japanese, so the card name and effects won't be in English
    Card backs are identical to TCG, so they're compatible for display/collecting
    Exclusive OCG prints — some rare versions of Dark Magician Girl were only ever released in Japan and never got a TCG printing, so you may find versions here you literally can't buy back home

Just browse freely — everything there is OCG by default. The rarer/more expensive cards in the binders are often the Japan-exclusive prints worth grabbing while you're there.
